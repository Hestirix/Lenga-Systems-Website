# Replit Prompt — Refine Lenga Systems positioning, services, and team

Update the existing Lenga Systems website so the content accurately reflects the company we are building.

## Core company positioning

Lenga Systems is an **IT & AI Consultancy**.

It is not primarily a web-development or app-development company.

The company should be positioned as a technology and systems partner that helps businesses understand, design, implement, integrate, modernize, and manage the systems and technology they depend on.

AI consultancy and implementation must remain highly visible and should be treated as one of Lenga Systems' defining capabilities, not buried among many unrelated services.

The core idea is:

**We advise. We design. We implement. We support.**

Lenga should feel like a practical technology consultancy focused on delivery, not a creative agency or generic software-development shop.

---

# 1. Homepage

Preserve the existing visual design and layout.

Keep the existing hero direction around:

**IT & AI consultancy for the systems your business relies on**

Maintain the strong emphasis on:

* IT & Systems Consulting
* AI Consulting & Implementation
* Systems Integration
* Process Automation
* Digital Transformation

However, reduce the prominence of standalone **Website Development** and **Application Development** as primary company-defining services.

Websites and applications remain capabilities Lenga can deliver when needed, but they are implementation tools rather than the company's identity.

Where appropriate, reframe these services around business outcomes.

For example:

Instead of:
**Website Development**

Consider:
**Digital Platforms & Business Web Systems**

Instead of:
**Application Development**

Consider:
**Custom Business Systems & Applications**

Do not force these exact names if the existing layout works better with another professional wording, but preserve this strategic direction.
